Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Daily Board is a personal task-tracking app. This page explains what information it collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, an optional display name, and a hashed version of your password (we never store your password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, first name and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

We also store the tasks you create: titles, descriptions, priorities and which column they are in.

How we use it

Your information is used only to sign you in, keep you signed in, and show you your own tasks. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a MongoDB database. The app is hosted on Render. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and Google Sign-In is provided by Google.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your account and tasks deleted, email us at the address below and we will remove them.
